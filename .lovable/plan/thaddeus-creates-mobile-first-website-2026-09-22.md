# thaddeus creates — Mobile-first website

## Goal
Create a distinctive, welcoming showcase for **thaddeus creates**, focused on handmade custom gifts and an easy path from browsing to enquiry.

## Visual direction
- Use the selected **Petal & Pistachio** palette: soft blush, fresh green, warm near-white, and berry-toned text.
- Pair **Syne** for expressive headings with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for clear supporting text.
- Create a fresh playful-browse composition from scratch, independent of the discarded concepts.
- Keep the tone cute, modern, minimal, and handcrafted without becoming childish or resembling a standard gift-shop template.
- Use custom-generated product photography that clearly shows resin texture, stationery detail, satin flowers, chocolates, and photo customisation.

## Page structure
1. **Opening view** — prominent studio name, short custom-gifting message, one clear browse action, and an original product image.
2. **Creations gallery** — an asymmetric mobile-first grid featuring resin frames, keychains, thalis, wedding invitations, occasion cards, and satin bouquets.
3. **Personalisation** — concise examples of how names, photos, colours, finishes, and occasion details can be customised.
4. **Ordering steps** — a simple three-step explanation from sharing an idea through approval and handcrafting.
5. **Enquiry section** — an inviting contact area without inventing a phone number, social handle, pricing, delivery promise, or customer claim.
6. **Minimal footer** — business name and a warm handcrafted sign-off.

## Interaction and accessibility
- Make product exploration comfortable on small screens first, then expand cleanly for tablets and desktops.
- Add restrained tactile movement and pressed states, with reduced-motion support.
- Keep text readable, controls finger-friendly, images stable, and colour contrast accessible.
- Ensure navigation and enquiry actions use clear labels and keyboard focus states.

## Technical details
- Replace the blank home page at `/` and add page-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Define the selected colours and typography as reusable design tokens.
- Load web fonts from the document head and keep all visual styling theme-aware.
- Generate and bundle original imagery locally rather than hotlinking stock assets.
- Verify the finished page at mobile and desktop sizes, including layout, links, image loading, and console errors.

## Contact limitation
The supplied details include only the business name. The initial website will avoid fake contact information; the final enquiry destination can be connected when a WhatsApp number, Instagram handle, or email address is provided.
