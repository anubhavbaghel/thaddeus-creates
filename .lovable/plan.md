# Animated real-work hero

## Goal
Keep the homepage hero image area’s current dimensions, crop, label, and overall layout, but turn it into an automatically moving stack of real-work photos.

## Changes
- Use a curated mix of the existing real resin and bouquet photographs in the hero.
- Present the photos as layered cards within the current image footprint.
- At a calm interval, move the front card backward and bring the next image forward from behind.
- Use subtle depth, rotation, and easing so the motion feels handcrafted rather than like a standard slideshow.
- Pause while the visitor points at or focuses the image area, and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Keep the sequence accessible with meaningful alternative text and no layout shift.

## Verification
- Check the animation and image framing on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm the hero retains its current size and does not create page overflow.
- Confirm reduced-motion mode shows a stable image without automatic movement.
