# thaddeus creates — Complete catalogue website

## Goal
Expand the current homepage into a polished, mobile-first catalogue website where visitors can explore the full range, understand personalisation, and enquire about a custom piece.

## Typography
- Keep **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy.
- Replace the heavy Syne headings with **Urbanist** in light and medium weights: calm, minimal, gently distinctive, and fully sans-serif.
- Reduce oversized headline weight, open the line spacing, and retain one restrained blush underline as the handcrafted accent.

## Pages
1. **Home** — retain the current opening and gallery direction, add featured categories, personalisation, ordering steps, studio introduction, and clear links into the catalogue.
2. **Creations** — a filterable visual catalogue covering resin frames, keychains, celebration thalis, invitations and cards, and satin bouquets.
3. **Creation detail** — reusable individual pages with imagery, customisation possibilities, suitable occasions, making notes, and an enquiry path; no invented prices or stock claims.
4. **About** — a concise studio story focused on thoughtful, personal, handmade work without inventing biography or customer claims.
5. **Contact** — an enquiry-ready page that explains what details to share. Until a real WhatsApp number, email, or social handle is supplied, the action will clearly remain unavailable rather than using fake contact information.

## Shared experience
- Add a consistent compact header, mobile menu, active navigation, and full footer across every page.
- Preserve the Petal & Pistachio palette and original product photography.
- Use the two new original images for resin keychains and celebration thalis alongside the existing four images.
- Make catalogue cards link to useful detail pages and keep controls keyboard accessible and touch friendly.
- Keep animation quiet and respect reduced-motion settings.

## Technical details
- Create shared navigation, footer, catalogue data, and product-card building blocks so every page stays visually consistent.
- Add a route for each main page and a dynamic route for catalogue details.
- Give every page unique title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Verify navigation, catalogue filtering, detail links, image loading, contact states, mobile layout, desktop layout, and browser errors.
